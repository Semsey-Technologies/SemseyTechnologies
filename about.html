About Semsey Technologies

Who I Am

I’m Don — a solo developer, researcher, and systems thinker. I build tools, models, and experiments that help people understand and shape the systems around them. I don’t come from a traditional engineering background; everything I know comes from curiosity, iteration, and refusing to accept “that’s just how it works” as an answer.

My work spans software, theory, and practical problem‑solving. If something can be made clearer, faster, or more humane, I try to build it.

What I Build

Semsey Technologies is my umbrella for everything I create:

  • RTS Device Suite Pro — a modular, privacy‑first toolkit for real‑world systems.
  • Open models and frameworks — theories and structures for understanding behavior, incentives, and decision‑making.
  • Experiments — prototypes, simulations, and tools that test ideas in the real world.
  • Documentation and writing — clear explanations, transparent reasoning, and practical guidance.

Everything I build follows the same principles: modular, predictable, privacy‑respecting, and empowering for the user.

Why I Build It

Most systems — technical or human — are harder to understand than they need to be. They hide their logic, bury their settings, and assume the user should adapt to the system instead of the other way around.

I build the opposite.

My goal is to create tools and models that:

  • make complexity understandable
  • give people control over their own systems
  • remove friction instead of adding it
  • stay transparent, honest, and non‑restrictive

If something I build helps someone think more clearly or work more effectively, then it’s doing its job.

How I Work

I work in focused bursts. When something grabs my attention, I can break it apart, map it out, and document every corner of it with obsessive clarity. Other times, I move fast, build the thing, and come back to clean it up later. My process isn’t linear — it’s iterative, adaptive, and driven by whatever problem needs solving right now.

I don’t pretend to be perfectly organized. I’m not. But I am consistent about one thing: I always push the work forward.

Whether that means deep‑dive documentation or rapid prototyping depends on the day, the problem, and the hyperfocus of the moment.

Use What Works for You

Everything here is designed to be used freely. Take what helps you, ignore what doesn’t, and adapt anything to your own needs. My work is meant to be a resource, not a gate.

Transparency & Expectations

I’m one person. I move fast, I break things occasionally, and I fix them just as quickly. I don’t pretend to have all the answers — but I do share everything I learn along the way.

If you want to collaborate, ask questions, or explore an idea, I’m always open to conversations that lead somewhere interesting.
