Retro 1990s/2000s Desktop & Internet Simulator

A historical-computing environment recreating the visual language and interaction patterns of late-1990s and early-2000s personal computing without emulating legacy CPU hardware. Its planned environment includes a desktop shell, browser recreation, internal email world, and synchronized television scheduler.

An interactive recreation of using a home computer in the late 1990s and early 2000s. It recreates the desktop and internet experience rather than pretending to run the original computer hardware.
