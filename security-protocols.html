Multi-Persona Security Protocol

A practical protocol for creating and operating separate online personas while reducing identity correlation, information leakage, and cross-contamination. It includes threat-model planning, persona templates, operational rules, communication practices, and incident response guidance.

A guide to keeping separate online identities from becoming accidentally connected. It covers planning, account separation, everyday habits, and what to do if something goes wrong.

Claudia Lock Protocol (CLP)

A proposed zero-trust physical-digital security framework for controlled and potentially air-gapped environments. The concept explores physical authorization and access-control mechanisms; implementation and security guarantees are not established by the current portfolio material.

A security concept exploring how physical controls could help protect sensitive information, including in environments kept separate from the internet. It should not be treated as a proven or finished security product.
