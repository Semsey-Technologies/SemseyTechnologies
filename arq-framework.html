Conceptual Framework

Adaptive Readiness Quotient (ARQ)

A Functional Framework for World Readiness

The ARQ is designed to assess an individual’s functional capacity to manage complex, multi-variable life systems (work, relationships, and self-management). It posits that readiness is dimensional and asynchronous—competence varies significantly regardless of chronological age.

Domain 1: Self-System Management

Evaluates executive function, long-term foresight, and the ability to maintain a sustainable personal system.

Metric Universal Question Level 4 Focus (Insight)
Financial Foresight "You get your monthly money. How do you decide what to do with it so you are happy next month and next year?" Identifies saving goals, prioritizes needs vs. wants, and accounts for contingency costs.
Health & Balance "You are super busy all week. What do you stop doing, and what do you make sure you still do, so you don't burn out?" Prioritizes non-negotiable wellness (sleep, food) and strategically cuts low-impact commitments.
Self Accountability "You have a goal to start something new. You fail three days in a row. What do you do on day four?" Analyzes cause of failure, revises the plan, and sets a smaller, feasible step to re-engage.

Domain 2: Social and Relationship Systems

Evaluates emotional intelligence, empathy, and the understanding of relational systems.

Metric Universal Question Level 4 Focus (Insight)
Relational Foresight "You have a big disagreement. If you win the fight but damage the relationship, what is the real cost?" Focuses on the long-term systemic value of the relationship over the short-term satisfaction of being "right."
Conflict Resolution "Someone close to you is upset with you. How do you talk to them to solve it and prevent recurrence?" Prioritizes deep listening, validates feelings, and proposes a process change.
Boundary Management "A friend needs help every weekend, but you need time for yourself. How do you solve this?" Articulates a clear, respectful boundary that protects both self and the relationship.

Domain 3: Work/Productivity Systems

Assesses the ability to handle complexity, manage pressure, and align actions with purpose.

Metric Universal Question Level 4 Focus (Insight)
Complexity Management "You have five urgent tasks, but only time for three. How do you decide, and what happens to the rest?" Prioritizes by consequence of failure and goal alignment. Communicates deferrals clearly.
Crisis Immunity "A major mistake ruined a month of work. Panicked people surround you. What are your next steps?" Separates emotion from data, assesses reality, and defines the minimum viable salvage action.
Purpose Alignment "Imagine you had all the money you ever needed. What would you do for the rest of your life?" Reveals if activities are driven by internal values and impact or by external consumption.

The ARQ Level System

Each metric is scored on a 1-4 scale, replacing age as the primary metric of readiness.

1
Reactive

Decisions driven by immediate mood or short-term desire. Low causal foresight.

2
Conventional

Driven by external compliance, social acceptance, or superficial planning.

3
Strategic

Analytical risk assessment and medium-term (3-5 year) personal planning.

4
Systemic

Holistic foresight, long-term system health, and principled, adaptive action.
