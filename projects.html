Perpetually Evolving Systems & Working-Slice Architecture Ideas and Systems That Keep Growing

Most projects here remain permanently open to iteration—expanding and branching as new edge cases, test logs, and architectural insights emerge rather than being frozen as static releases. For projects in active development, the approach emphasizes building concrete, functional slices that can be tested and verified, even when the broader architecture contains hundreds of planned features. Proposed concepts and research remain clearly identified as such.

These projects are designed to grow and improve over time. Instead of trying to build everything at once, active software projects are developed in smaller working pieces that can be tested. Ideas and proposals are clearly distinguished from software that has actually been built.
