Transparency & Expectations

I build a lot — theories, tools, experiments, prototypes, and occasionally full products. Some of it is polished. Some of it is experimental. Some of it is held together with duct tape and a good attitude.

Because I work independently and release everything openly:

  • I cannot guarantee production‑ready stability
  • I cannot provide ongoing support
  • All software is provided as‑is, with no warranties

I label what’s stable, experimental, or mostly broken. I document what I know and what I’m still figuring out. I improve things when I can. I never pretend something is more mature than it is.
