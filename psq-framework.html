Conceptual Framework

Problem Solving Quotient (PSQ)

Measuring Applied Adaptive Intelligence

The PSQ is a novel framework designed to measure an individual's adaptive and applied problem-solving ability in real-world contexts. Unlike traditional IQ tests, PSQ prioritizes dynamic thinking, strategic execution, and constraint navigation.

The Six Core Pillars

PSQ evaluates performance across six critical dimensions of practical intelligence:

01

Problem Identification

Detecting and clarifying the root issue rather than reacting to symptoms.

02

Strategic Thinking

Creating structured, goal-oriented approaches to complex challenges.

03

Creative Innovation

Generating novel solutions within strict operational or technical constraints.

04

Constraint Navigation

Operating effectively with limited time, resources, or available options.

05

Implementation

Executing and adapting plans in real-time as new data emerges.

06

Collaborative Intelligence

Coordinating problem-solving efforts with others effectively.

Assessment Scenarios

Sample scenarios used to evaluate the pillars through judgment and foresight.

Strategic Thinking

Scenario: You're launching a new product with minimal budget and limited time.

"Outline your strategy. What would you prioritize, and how would you measure success?"

Constraint Navigation

Scenario: A critical bug must be fixed quickly with limited code changes allowed.

"How do you prioritize fixes while ensuring stability and managing team stress?"

Implementation

Scenario: A process change you developed meets significant resistance from your team.

"How do you implement it across departments, handle pushback, and sustain results?"

Scoring & Evaluation Criteria

  • Clarity of Thought: How well is the root issue understood?
  • Logic and Flow: Is the reasoning coherent and systematic?
  • Creativity: Are solutions original and contextually relevant?
  • Feasibility: Are proposals realistic and actionable?
  • Execution Plan: Is there evidence of practical follow-through?

Conclusion & Invitation

This framework is offered as a prototype for clinicians, researchers, or educational professionals interested in exploring a more nuanced evaluation of adaptive intelligence. Feedback and partnership for formal validation is welcomed.
